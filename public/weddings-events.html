Common Questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings we recommend booking 6–12 months in advance, especially for peak season (May–October). For smaller events and quinceañeras, 2–3 months is typically enough. We do accept short-notice bookings when our calendar allows.

Do you travel outside Wine Country?

Yes. We have no travel fees within Sonoma, Marin, and Napa Counties. For destination bookings we charge travel and lodging at cost. Reach out and we’ll put together a custom quote.

When will I receive my photos and film?

You’ll get a sneak-peek gallery within 7 days of your event. Full edited photos and your cinematic film are delivered within 4–6 weeks for weddings, and 2–3 weeks for smaller events.

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. The packages here are starting points. We regularly customize for couples and clients who want to add hours, second shooters, additional video, or trim things they don’t need. Book a discovery call and we’ll design a package around your day.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We require a 25% retainer to lock the date, and the remaining balance can be split into 2–3 payments leading up to your event. We’ll lay out the schedule clearly when you book.

Ready to start your project?

Book a free 20-minute discovery call. We’ll talk through your vision and put a custom plan together — no pressure.
